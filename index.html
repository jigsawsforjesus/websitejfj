Our story

Puzzles with meaning, made for sharing

Jigsaws for Jesus began with a simple idea: create beautiful puzzles that spark faith-filled conversations around the kitchen table. Every puzzle features original artwork — chosen or created with care. These are not generic designs, but art you will want to frame when the last piece clicks into place.

Whether you are shopping for yourself, sending a gift, or ordering something uniquely personal, we treat every request like it matters — because it does.

“Slow down, gather loved ones, and let a puzzle become a moment of peace.”

— The heart behind every Jigsaws for Jesus design
